## LKS Dialer v1.9.4 — Phase 2: WebRTC Auto-Reconnection & State Reset

### 🚀 Key Improvements in v1.9.4

1. **Intelligent Network Reconnection (WiFi <-> 4G / Signal Drops)**
   - Monitored IceConnectionState transitions with automated timeout handling.
   - On DISCONNECTED: Displays "Reconnecting..." and activates a 15-second grace window to allow network handoffs without dropping calls.
   - On FAILED: Initiates automated estartIce() and SDP renegotiation to reconstruct the P2P or TURN path.
   - On CONNECTED / COMPLETED: Automatically cancels timeout timers and returns call to "Connected • WebRTC".

2. **WebRtcEngine State Isolation & Reset Protection**
   - Fixed race conditions where ending one call and initiating or answering a consecutive call could trigger a delayed idle reset.
   - Added esetIdleJob and econnectJob tracking with atomic cancellation on incoming/outgoing call attachment.
   - Guaranteed full teardown of media tracks, video capturers, and surface texture helpers between calls.
