## LKS Dialer v1.9.6 — Phase 4: Dynamic Adaptive Video & Live Quality UI

### 🚀 Key Improvements in v1.9.6

1. **Real-time WebRTC Quality Monitor**
   - Continuously samples WebRTC candidate-pair and inbound RTP stats (Round Trip Time, packet loss ratio, jitter).
   - Dynamically translates network metrics into a 5-bar signal indicator (
etworkQualityBars).
   - Integrated into both Audio and Video call screens with responsive color coding (Green / Amber / Red).

2. **Network-Adaptive Video Bitrate & Framerate**
   - Dynamically adjusts video RTP sender encoding parameters based on connection health:
     - 5 bars: 2.0 Mbps @ 30fps (Crystal-clear HD)
     - 4 bars: 1.2 Mbps @ 30fps (Smooth HD)
     - 3 bars: 800 Kbps @ 24fps (Standard Definition)
     - 2 bars: 400 Kbps @ 15fps (Bandwidth Saver)
     - 1 bar:  200 Kbps @ 15fps (Survival mode, prioritizing audio packets)
   - Prevents video freeze, lag, and audio packet loss on constrained mobile data or weak WiFi.

3. **In-Call Weak Network Warning Banners**
   - Prominently displays \"⚠️ Weak network\" notifications when quality drops below 2 bars so users know why call fidelity may be reduced.
